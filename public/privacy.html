Privacy Policy

HeheChat ("we", "us") is operated by NoFuture, c/o Autorenglück #81007, Albert-Einstein-Straße 47, 02977 Hoyerswerda, Germany. This policy explains what data HeheChat processes and why.

Twitch Login

HeheChat authenticates via Twitch OAuth. Your Twitch access token and account information are used to read chat, moderate channels, and display your Twitch profile — they are not shared with third parties.

Chat & Alert Data

Twitch chat messages and stream events (alerts, donations, follows, etc.) are processed in your browser to power the app's features. Emote metadata is cached locally in your browser (IndexedDB) for performance.

Backend Profile Sync

Your app settings (channels, alert configuration, visibility options) are stored on our backend, keyed to your account, so your configuration persists across devices. This data is used solely to provide the app's functionality.

No Advertising or Tracking

HeheChat does not use advertising trackers or sell user data.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to request deletion of your data, contact nofuture2077@posteo.de.
